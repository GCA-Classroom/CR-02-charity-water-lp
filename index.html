Our Mission

Charity: water is dedicated to bringing clean, safe drinking water and sanitation to communities across Africa, South Asia, and South America. We believe that access to clean water is the foundation for better health, greater opportunity, and long‑term prosperity. By investing in sustainable water solutions, we help families build stronger futures and unlock the potential of entire communities.

Stories of Change

Helen's Story

Helen used to spend hours each day walking long distances just to collect a small amount of water for her family. Clean water was scarce, and every day meant choosing between cooking, washing, or drinking. After her community gained access to clean water, everything changed — her children could attend school, she had time to work and care for her home, and her family's health and opportunities grew. Today, Helen runs small businesses, farms, and supports her grandchildren, all made possible by reliable access to clean water.
