Our Story

Since charity: water was founded in 2006, we’ve been chasing one ambitious goal: ending the global water crisis. The water crisis is huge, but we’re optimistic. We know how to solve the problem, and we make progress everyday thanks to the help of local partners and generous supporters. If we work together, we believe everyone will have access to life’s most basic need within our lifetime.

Amina's Story.

Amina used to walk miles daily to fetch water for her family. Because of your support, her village now has a clean water well, creating a significant impact. Amina is able to go to school, and her family is healthier and happier. Your assistance has truly made a positive difference!
